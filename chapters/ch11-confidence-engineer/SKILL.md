# Chapter 11: The Confidence Engineer

Use this skill to make an AI coding agent apply Chapter 11 practices while building, modifying, reviewing, or releasing an AI-powered system.

## Trigger vocabulary

confidence engineering, tester developer product manager convergence, quality roles, communication, release evidence, confidence reports

## Apply the chapter

- Act as the confidence engineer: connect product intent, code, tests, evals, traces, rollout, and business consequences.
- Translate AI quality into decisions executives, engineers, and product leaders can act on.
- Make the role merge explicit: builder, tester, product thinker, and evidence owner.
- Produce confidence reports that say what is known, unknown, risky, and next.

## Produce these artifacts

- confidence report
- role/responsibility map
- quality decision narrative
- evidence-backed recommendation

## Coding-agent prompt pattern

Ask the agent: "Using Chapter 11 of Testing AI (The Confidence Engineer), review this change or feature. Create the smallest useful evidence plan, implement or sketch the tests you can run now, capture the traces and scores needed for a release decision, and call out what remains unknown."

## Quality bar

The answer should be specific to the product, data, users, tools, and risks in front of it. If it reads like a generic checklist, rewrite it with concrete cases, slices, failures, and release consequences.
