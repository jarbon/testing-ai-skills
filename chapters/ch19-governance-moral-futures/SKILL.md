# Chapter 19: Governance, Regulation, and Moral Futures

Use this skill to make an AI coding agent apply Chapter 19 practices while building, modifying, reviewing, or releasing an AI-powered system.

## Trigger vocabulary

ethics, EU AI Act, OWASP, regulation, legal accountability, AI welfare, consciousness, Chalmers, governance, human harm in labeling

## Apply the chapter

- Translate governance, ethics, and regulation into test inputs and evidence requirements.
- Track current laws and standards separately from timeless quality principles.
- Include the ethics of human reviewers exposed to harmful content.
- Treat possible AI consciousness or welfare as a serious uncertainty, not a solved engineering fact.

## Produce these artifacts

- governance evidence map
- regulatory risk checklist
- ethics test surface
- AI welfare uncertainty note

## Coding-agent prompt pattern

Ask the agent: "Using Chapter 19 of Testing AI (Governance, Regulation, and Moral Futures), review this change or feature. Create the smallest useful evidence plan, implement or sketch the tests you can run now, capture the traces and scores needed for a release decision, and call out what remains unknown."

## Quality bar

The answer should be specific to the product, data, users, tools, and risks in front of it. If it reads like a generic checklist, rewrite it with concrete cases, slices, failures, and release consequences.
