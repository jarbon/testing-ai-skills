## Chapter Guidance

## Overview

Developers will not disappear into prompting. The valuable developer becomes the person who manages coding agents well enough to make generation reliable.

That means directing agents to build harnesses, replay systems, custom judges, trace inspectors, data contracts, rollout gates, red-team suites, synthetic case generators, and dashboards that show uncertainty instead of hiding it. The human work becomes specification, supervision, review, orchestration, and validation.

The statistical bar also rises. A developer does not need to derive every formula, but they do need to know when an average is misleading, when a sample is too small, when a slice is underrepresented, when a p-value is not permission, and when a new high-water mark is just variance.

## Expert Notes

The role shift is practical, not mystical. Developers will still need judgment, architecture taste, debugging skill, and communication, but the work increasingly becomes orchestration and validation of agent-built systems.
