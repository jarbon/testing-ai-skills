# Embodied and Long-Running AI Systems

**Book location:** Chapter 18  
**Use when:** humanoid robot, robotics simulation, perception, navigation, physical safety, sim-to-real, dangerous physical embodied, embodied robotics safety real environments, embodied robotics simulation virtual world, embodied robotics planning navigation recovery, latency, embodied robotics power latency cost, embodied robotics human interaction acceptance, sensor fusion  
**Source:** [*Testing AI: Engineering Confidence in Non-Deterministic Systems* by Jason Arbon](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0H8J9GCK1)

## Actions

- Test localization and navigation.
- Test human-robot interaction.
- Test operator authentication, least-privilege access, explicit activation indicators, geographic and contractual restrictions, session logging, emergency revocation, and the robot's behavior when the remote connection is slow or lost.
- Treat this memory as a high-value data system, not as disposable telemetry.
- Start with the action inventory.
- Test permission boundaries.
- Use physical rate limits and hard constraints.
- Do not rely only on the model's judgment when a mechanical limit, spend cap, geofence, speed limit, or emergency stop can reduce harm.

## Evidence to Produce

Produce a decision-oriented evidence package: system and population, cases and slices, measurements and uncertainty, severe failures, known blind spots, and a ship, canary, hold, rollback, or collect-more-evidence recommendation.
